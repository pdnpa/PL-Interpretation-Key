## Identification criteria

Bare land covers exposed substrates with little or no vegetation, including bare ground, bare rock, unconsolidated sediments (sand/mud/shingle), exposed peat, and scree. It is typically recognised by strong textural signatures and landform context (coastal, fluvial, upland), and can be highly dynamic where shaped by erosion, disturbance, flooding, or storms.
