## Identification criteria

Grassland includes a spectrum from intensively managed improved pasture to semi-natural rough, neutral, acid, and calcareous grasslands. From above, improved grassland is typically smooth and uniform with clear field management patterns, while semi-natural grasslands show greater internal texture and patchiness linked to soils, slope, grazing intensity, and succession at margins.
