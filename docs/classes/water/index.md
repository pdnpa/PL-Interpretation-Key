## Identification criteria

Water includes inland open water and flowing watercourses such as rivers and streams. In aerial imagery, water is defined by smooth reflective/dark surfaces and clear channel or shoreline geometry, with seasonal variability in extent and appearance driven by flow, drawdown, turbidity, and algal blooms.
