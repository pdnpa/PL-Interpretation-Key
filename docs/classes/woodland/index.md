## Identification criteria

Woodland & Scrubland includes tree and shrub-dominated habitats from continuous woodland canopy to scrub and linear hedgerows. Aerially, it is recognised by canopy texture, structure, and edge form (blocks, belts, lines), with broadleaved habitats showing strong seasonal leaf-on/leaf-off change and conifers remaining more consistent year-round.
